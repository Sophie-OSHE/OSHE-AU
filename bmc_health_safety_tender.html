BMC integrates health and safety, site management, and operational delivery into a single digitally enabled platform through OSHE. This document outlines our approach across all stages of the development — from early works through to staged handover — with clear, measurable outcomes at every level.
Our Approach

Eight Pillars of H&S Delivery

01

H&S Management System

All health and safety processes are executed and recorded within OSHE, ensuring consistent application of standards across all stages of the development. The system is designed to scale across multi-stage delivery, maintaining control as work fronts expand and sequencing evolves.

Outcome

Consistent, scalable system — reduces fragmentation and supports efficient staged delivery.

02

Site Induction & Workforce Alignment

All workers and subcontractors complete site-specific inductions through OSHE prior to site access, with full compliance required before mobilisation. Inductions are updated as staging and site conditions change. Workforce competency, training, and access are controlled through the system.

Outcome

Improves mobilisation efficiency and ensures consistent workforce alignment across all stages.

03

Risk Management & Task Analysis

BMC manages risk at the point of work through structured Task Analysis within OSHE. All active work fronts must have a current Task Analysis before commencement, updated in real time to reflect staging and installation activities. Supervisors verify controls in the field before work proceeds.

Outcome

Reduces trade clashes, installation errors, and rework — directly supporting cost control.

04

Staging & Interface Risk Management

BMC manages staging and interface risk through clear separation of active work zones, controlled access aligned to stage boundaries, and real-time risk communication through OSHE. Risks associated with shared accessways, services, and adjacent works are actively managed as stages progress.

Outcome

Maintains workflow continuity between stages and supports programme certainty.

05

Site Reviews & Assurance

BMC applies a risk-based assurance model aligned to project complexity, with increased review frequency for early stage establishment, high-risk activities, prefabricated installations, and stage interface zones. All reviews are completed within OSHE with actions tracked to close-out.

Outcome

Improves quality of installation and reduces recurring issues and downstream cost impacts.

06

Incident Management & Continuous Learning

All incidents, near misses, and hazards are reported and managed through OSHE with immediate visibility and structured follow-up. Incidents are investigated promptly, with corrective actions tracked within the system. Learnings are fed back into Task Analysis, inductions, and site briefings.

Outcome

Prevents repeat issues, reduces disruption, and protects programme delivery.

07

Contractor & Supply Chain Management

All subcontractors are assessed prior to engagement, inducted before site access, and monitored through live performance data in OSHE. Performance is tracked across Task Analysis completion, site reviews, and incident reporting, ensuring consistent standards across all trades.

Outcome

Reduces variability between subcontractors and supports efficient sequencing across all stages.

08

Leadership, Resources & External Support

BMC provides dedicated H&S leadership through full-time Health, Safety and Environmental Manager Darryl Dawson. This is supported by OSHE, providing external resource and expertise in managing large, staged construction projects with multiple concurrent work fronts.

Outcome

Ensures sufficient capability as the project scales, maintaining performance under programme pressure.
