OSHE Platform

Everything your site needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

9 core features. One platform. Works on any device and simple enough that your team will use it from day one.

01

Contractor Management

Pre-qual applications, insurance tracking, renewal alerts, and a full contractor register.

02

QR Sign In and Out

Workers and visitors scan a QR code with their phone camera. No app needed for site sign-in.

03

Job Management and SWMS

Safe Work Method Statements built in minutes, not hours. Pre-loaded risk assessments and a guided questionnaire do the heavy lifting.

04

Incident Reporting

Workers flag incidents at sign-out. The right people notified instantly. Actions tracked to closure.

05

Inductions and Training

Baseline modules included. Fully customisable on top. Two-step sign-off keeps everything accountable.

06

JSA — Job Safety Analysis

Create, complete and sign JSAs on any device. Pre-loaded by trade and task type. Linked to your SWMS. Works offline on mobile.

07

Hazard and Safety Registers

Hazard, PPE, plant and equipment, and hazardous substances. Pre-loaded library. Full audit history. Download as print-ready documents.

08

Smart Notifications

Insurance renewals, training due dates, contractor re-prequalifications. Nothing falls through the cracks.

09

Mobile App

Works offline. Built for site, not the office. Fill in forms, log hazards, complete JSAs, report incidents. Everything syncs when you get back in range.
